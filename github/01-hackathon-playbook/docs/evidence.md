## Run 1 · toolkit standards (2026-09-21): Hackathon strategy and pitching

Full report: [`technical-hackathon-toolkit-2026-09-21/research.md`](../../_research/technical-hackathon-toolkit-2026-09-21/research.md)

- **What wins:** clarity over complexity; one feature done well; the problem up front; working within about 90 s; mock slow or flaky parts [T1]. Named Devpost judges list what kills a submission: backend-only projects with no UI, ambiguity, barely changed templates, recycled projects, and unequal team contribution [T2]. Some judges look at visual appeal first [T2].
- **Criteria:**
  - Devpost's common set has no weights: implementation, ease of use, demo, impact, idea quality, design [T3].
  - ETHGlobal: Technicality, Originality, Practicality, Usability, WOW [T5].
  - MLH science fair: about 3 min per team, stack-ranked top 3 [T7].
  - One unverified snippet says MLH doesn't judge pitch or idea quality. That contradicts sponsor-judge practice, so read each event's rubric (low confidence).
- **Video and demo:**
  - Devpost: allow 2–3 h before the deadline; videos are usually under 3 min; write your own script [T4].
  - ETHGlobal: 2–4 min, at least 720p, intro of 20 s or less, no TTS or AI voiceover, no phone recording, no sped-up footage [T5].
  - MLH digital events: 2 min or less, naming the event [T6].
- **Gaps:** no fetched primary source on which AI tools are used at hackathons, and no hour-by-hour timelines from winners.

## Run 1 · toolkit standards (2026-09-21): Product plumbing and free credits

Full report: [`technical-hackathon-toolkit-2026-09-21/research.md`](../../_research/technical-hackathon-toolkit-2026-09-21/research.md)

*Deepen 2026-09-22.*

- **Analytics and errors:**
  - PostHog free: 1M events, 5K session replays and 100K exceptions a month, no card [T85].
  - Vercel Web Analytics on Hobby: 50K events and no custom events [T86].
- **Payments:** Stripe sandboxes simulate payments with test card 4242 4242 4242 4242. Using real cards for testing is prohibited [T87].
- **Maps:**
  - Mapbox free: 50K web map loads and 100K geocoding and directions requests [T88].
  - OpenFreeMap: keyless and unlimited tiles for MapLibre, attribution required [T89].
- **Email:** Resend free gives 3K emails a month, capped at 100 a day [T90].
- **Credits:** the GitHub Student Developer Pack includes Azure $100, Copilot, Codespaces, free domains, MongoDB Atlas $50, Sentry and Stripe $25 [T91] (medium confidence on the exact offer wording).
- **Not found:** Sentry's standalone free-plan limits, Google Maps free usage, and AI-vendor hackathon credit programmes.
