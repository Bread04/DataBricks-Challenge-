DengueRadar — Round 1 Judging-Fit Forge

Pressure-tested against DAISI's own rubric: Problem fit & social impact 30% · Solution quality & originality 30% · Data & technical feasibility 25% · Clarity 15% — against ~140 competing teams.

Outcome

Six branches opened, six driven to a locked decision, plus two factual gaps closed outright. The submission's argument no longer leans on the forecast/map (table stakes per the brief) or on a model result that doesn't exist yet — it leans on a data-sourcing constraint the team actually solved, a prescriptive layer that's honest about what it can and can't route, and a validation methodology presented as designed rigor rather than a fabricated number.

Locked decisions

Lock

No institutional routing claim. Actions are framed as a checklist any engaged resident, RC volunteer, or MCST council member can act on — not an alert routed to a specific Town Council/RC/MCST, since planning-area boundaries don't map to those institutions and no dataset bridges the two.

Lock

Top 3 drivers → stacked, deduplicated, ranked actions — not one leading driver collapsed to one action. Fixes a real self-contradiction already sitting in docs/model-definitions.md §6.

Lock

Second routing axis: cause, not just driver. NEA's own cluster feed tags breeding source (HOMES / PUBLIC_PLACES / CONSTRUCTION_SITES). Construction-site clusters route to NEA/BCA — nationally scoped, so it doesn't reopen the routing lock above. The field is sparse (2 of 11 clusters), so this is a bonus layer with an honest "root cause not yet identified" fallback, not the backbone.

Lock

Round 1's proof point is the validation methodology (rolling-origin testing, leave-areas-out, cost-matrix threshold) — not a model result. No task exists before 4 Oct to actually train the model or run a backtest; that work is a Build Sprint deliverable, gated on being shortlisted.

Lock

The idea-originality claim is the data wall, not the forecast. Official Singapore open data has no per-area historical dengue record. DengueRadar's original move was finding and validating two citable archives (SGCharts 2015–2020, Nature Scientific Data 2022) to build the training history the brief requires — claimed as "we solved the constraint," not "we're the only ones who could."

Lock

The whole submission argues idea + solution + execution soundness — never a fabricated performance number, anywhere in the deck.

Resolved fact-check

2020 dengue case count = 35,261 — reproduced directly from analysis/data/weekly_idb.csv. The competing 35,012 figure has no traceable source and should be dropped.

Resolved fact-check

The climate "why now" line cites the official DAISI brief directly — cheapest possible source. Caveat: the team's own research shows the relationship is more nuanced than "hot + rainy = worse" (temperature above 31°C often dampens risk; heavy flushing rain lowers it), and the model design already reflects this correctly — don't contradict it elsewhere.
